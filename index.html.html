SkinBloom AI - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

SkinBloom AI ("the app", "we") is a skincare companion app by Minahil Shabir. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. Contact: minahilshabir153@gmail.com.

In short: most of your information stays on your phone. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads. If you use the AI Coach, the messages you send are processed by our server and an AI service provider so it can reply. Your photos never leave your phone.

1. Information the app uses

2. AI Coach and service providers

When you send a message to the AI Coach, the text of your recent messages is sent to our server, which forwards it to a third-party AI service provider to write the reply.

If "Personalize the AI Coach" is switched on (you can change this at any time in the app under Profile > Privacy & data), the request also includes your skin profile, your product list and your journal check-ins from the last week, including notes you wrote. Photos are never sent.

Please do not put information in your messages or notes that you would not want processed in this way (for example other people's personal details).

Other service providers we use:

  • Google Firebase (sign-in and, if enabled, cloud backup).
  • A cloud hosting provider to run our server. Standard technical logs (such as IP address and time of request) may be kept by the hosting provider.
  • Google ML Kit text recognition, which runs on your phone.

3. How we use information

We use information only to provide the features above, keep the service safe and limit misuse. We do not sell your information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not share it with data brokers.

4. Keeping and deleting your data

  • On your phone: in the app go to Profile > Privacy & data > "Delete all data on this phone". Uninstalling the app also removes local data.
  • Your account: go to Profile > Delete account. This deletes your sign-in account and, if you used it, your cloud backup.
  • Server counters: the anonymous usage counter is kept only as long as needed to enforce daily limits.
  • You can also email us at minahilshabir153@gmail.com to ask about your data.

The AI provider and hosting provider handle data under their own policies and retention rules.

5. Your choices

  • Use the app without an account.
  • Turn "Personalize the AI Coach" on or off at any time.
  • Export your journal from Profile > Privacy & data.
  • Turn off notifications in your phone settings.
  • Choose not to add photos.

6. Children

SkinBloom AI is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you are under 18, please use the app together with a parent or guardian and speak to a doctor about acne or ongoing skin problems.

7. Health notice

SkinBloom AI provides general skincare information and habit tracking. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. The Glow Score is a fun habit score based on your own inputs, not a medical measurement. Always seek advice from a qualified doctor or dermatologist for medical concerns. If you have bleeding, pus, a quickly spreading rash, swelling of the face or lips, fever, severe pain or trouble breathing, get medical help promptly or contact local emergency services.

8. Security

Data on your phone is stored in the app's private storage. Data sent to our server is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). No system is perfectly secure, so please take care with what you enter.

9. International processing

Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
